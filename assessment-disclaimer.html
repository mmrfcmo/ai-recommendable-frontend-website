Important Assessment Disclaimer

Recommendable by AI does not guarantee that any business will be discovered, recommended, ranked, cited, included or otherwise represented by any AI system, search engine, platform or third-party service.

The assessment is designed to evaluate and identify areas relating to six Trust Signals that may influence how clearly and credibly a business is represented across its digital presence. It is an indicative diagnostic assessment, not a prediction or guarantee of future visibility, recommendation, ranking, traffic, enquiries or commercial performance.

The assessment considers information available from the business website and, where applicable, other accessible digital sources. Results may therefore be affected by the availability, accuracy, completeness and accessibility of that information at the time of assessment.

Five Trust Signals can be assessed through the automated website scanning process. The sixth signal, External Validation, cannot be reliably measured through an automated website scan alone. External Validation relates to independent authority, recognition, references, mentions and other third-party evidence surrounding a business. It therefore requires additional research and assessment beyond the automated scan.

Where External Validation is referenced in the assessment, it is presented as a methodological consideration rather than a numerical score or definitive measurement.

Any recommendations arising from the assessment are intended to identify potential areas for improvement. Implementing those recommendations does not guarantee a particular outcome, including AI discovery, AI recommendation, search visibility, rankings, citations, enquiries, leads, sales or revenue.

No representation or warranty is made that improving any individual Trust Signal, or all six Trust Signals collectively, will result in a particular outcome.

Our Services

Services & Client Outcomes

Every business has a digital footprint. AI systems use six key trust signals to decide whether to recommend you. Here is what each signal means, why it matters, and what happens when you fix it.

Service 1 of 6

Schema.org Markup

Structured Data Implementation

What it is: Schema.org markup is code added to your website that helps AI systems understand exactly what your business is, what you offer, and how you operate. It tells search engines and AI platforms: "This is a business, here is our address, here are our services, here are our reviews."

Why it matters: Without schema, AI systems have to guess what your business does. With schema, they know. Businesses with properly implemented schema markup are significantly more likely to appear in AI-generated answers, rich snippets, and voice search results.

Impact when fixed: AI systems can instantly identify your business type, location, services, operating hours, reviews, and pricing. Your discoverability score typically increases by 12-20 points.

Case Study

A regional law firm with four offices across the Midlands had no schema markup on their website. AI systems could not determine whether they were a solicitors' practice, a consultancy, or a legal directory. After implementing Organisation, LocalBusiness, and Service schema across all pages, their visibility in AI search results improved noticeably. Within three weeks, the firm reported a measurable increase in direct enquiries originating from AI-powered search tools. The fix took under two hours to implement.

Score uplift: 0/20 → 18/20 | Timeline: 1 day

Case Study

A family-run plumbing business in Surrey had been trading for over 35 years. Their website mentioned "established 1988" but their Google Business Profile showed "established 2010". Their address was listed as "Guildford" on one directory and "Godalming" on another. Their phone number appeared in three different formats across the web. After standardising their NAP to a single authoritative version and updating 12 directories, the business saw a significant improvement in local AI search visibility. Their Google Business Profile impressions increased meaningfully within two weeks.

Score uplift: 3/15 → 13/15 | Timeline: 3 days

Service 2 of 6

Entity Clarity & NAP Consistency

Name, Address, Phone Alignment

What it is: Entity clarity means AI systems can form a clear, coherent picture of who your business is. NAP (Name, Address, Phone) consistency means the same information appears everywhere your business is listed — on your website, Google, Yell, Facebook, and every other directory.

Why it matters: AI systems cross-reference your business information across multiple sources. If they find inconsistencies — different phone numbers, slightly different business names, conflicting addresses — they lose confidence in your entity. This directly reduces the likelihood of being recommended.

Impact when fixed: AI systems can confidently identify your business across all platforms. Inconsistent data is eliminated, and your business appears as a single, trustworthy entity. Typical score improvement of 8-12 points.

Service 3 of 6

Content Depth

Topic Coverage & Authority Building

What it is: Content depth measures how thoroughly your website covers the topics your customers care about. AI systems look for FAQs, guides, service pages, and structured content that demonstrates expertise in your field.

Why it matters: When a potential customer asks AI a question about your industry, the AI searches for the most authoritative, comprehensive answer. Businesses with shallow content get skipped. Businesses with deep, well-structured content get recommended.

Impact when fixed: Your website becomes a reference source that AI systems trust. Each new piece of relevant content strengthens your authority. Typical score improvement of 10-16 points.

Case Study

An independent estate agency in Kent had a website with only five pages and no FAQ section. AI systems had very little content to reference when evaluating their expertise. We added a comprehensive FAQ section covering the home-buying process, a guide to selling property, an area guide for the local market, and a buying vs renting comparison page. The agency's content depth score improved substantially. Within a month, their website was being referenced by AI platforms as a source for local property market information.

Score uplift: 4/20 → 16/20 | Timeline: 5 days

Case Study

A digital marketing agency in Manchester had excellent case studies and client testimonials but none of them were displayed on their website. They had a Trustpilot profile with a 4.7 rating but no link to it from their site. They had been featured in two industry publications but never mentioned it. We restructured their homepage to include a testimonials section, added a client logos bar, linked their Trustpilot profile, and created a dedicated case studies page. The agency's trust signal score improved significantly, and they reported that new clients began mentioning their Trustpilot rating as a factor in choosing them.

Score uplift: 3/15 → 14/15 | Timeline: 3 days

Service 4 of 6

Trust Signals

Reviews, Testimonials & Social Proof

What it is: Trust signals are the visible indicators that your business is credible and reputable. Reviews, testimonials, case studies, awards, accreditations, client logos, and media mentions all contribute to how AI systems evaluate your trustworthiness.

Why it matters: AI systems prioritise businesses with verifiable trust signals. A business with 50 positive reviews, a strong Trustpilot rating, and published case studies is far more likely to be recommended than a business with no visible social proof — even if their services are identical.

Impact when fixed: AI systems can immediately verify your credibility. Your business stands out from competitors who lack visible trust signals. Typical score improvement of 8-12 points.

Service 5 of 6

Technical SEO

Meta Tags, Mobile Optimisation & Site Health

What it is: Technical SEO covers the foundational elements that make your website accessible and understandable to both AI systems and users. Meta descriptions, viewport settings, heading structures, HTTPS security, and mobile responsiveness all play a role.

Why it matters: If AI systems cannot properly crawl and interpret your website, they cannot recommend it. Missing meta descriptions, broken heading structures, and poor mobile optimisation all reduce your chances of being discovered and recommended.

Impact when fixed: Your website becomes fully accessible to AI crawlers. Every page is properly structured, indexed, and understood. Typical score improvement of 8-12 points.

Case Study

A boutique hotel in the Lake District had a beautiful website that was nearly invisible to AI systems. The site had no meta descriptions on any page, no viewport tag (making it appear broken on mobile devices), and the heading structure was a flat list of generic divs rather than proper H1-H3 hierarchy. After a technical SEO audit, we added meta descriptions to all 14 pages, implemented a proper heading hierarchy, fixed the viewport issue, and ensured HTTPS was enforced. The hotel's technical SEO score improved dramatically, and they began appearing in AI-generated travel recommendations for the first time.

Score uplift: 2/15 → 14/15 | Timeline: 2 days

Case Study

A financial advisory firm in Bristol had a single-page website with a brief description of services and a contact form. They had no blog, no guides, no FAQ section, and no resources. AI systems had nothing to reference when evaluating their expertise. We developed a content strategy that included a 12-article pillar content plan, an FAQ section covering the 20 most common questions about financial planning, and a downloadable guide to retirement planning. Over three months, the firm's content depth score improved from 2/20 to 17/20. They began appearing in AI-generated responses to financial planning queries and reported an increase in qualified leads.

Score uplift: 2/20 → 17/20 | Timeline: 10 days

Service 6 of 6

Content Strategy

AI-Ready Content Planning & Creation

What it is: Content strategy goes beyond adding pages — it is a structured approach to creating the content that AI systems need to understand, evaluate, and recommend your business. This includes pillar content, topic clusters, FAQ schemas, and authority-building resources.

Why it matters: AI systems rank content by relevance, depth, and authority. A scattered approach to content creation leaves gaps in your topic coverage. A strategic approach ensures that every piece of content serves a purpose and strengthens your overall discoverability.

Impact when fixed: Your website becomes a comprehensive resource that AI systems reference with confidence. Topic coverage is complete, content is structured for AI consumption, and your authority in your field is clearly established. Typical score improvement of 12-18 points.

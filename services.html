SERVICES & CLIENT OUTCOMES

How we help strengthen your digital representation.

The starting point is understanding what your business currently communicates online — what is clear, what is missing, what is inconsistent, and where the supporting evidence is strongest or weakest.

From there, we can identify what is worth improving and help you work through those priorities in a structured way.

HOW WE WORK

Start with evidence. Then decide what is worth improving.

We don't begin with a list of things you should change. We begin by understanding the representation that already exists.

01

Assess

Establish how clearly and consistently your business is represented across the six Trust Signals.

02

Prioritise

Identify the gaps, inconsistencies and weaknesses that are most useful to address first.

03

Strengthen

Improve the underlying evidence, information and consistency where there is a clear reason to do so.

04

Review

Check the resulting representation and identify what should be maintained, refined or addressed next.

OUR SERVICES

Four stages. One straightforward process.

THE SIX TRUST SIGNALS

The areas we examine.

The assessment is built around six distinct signals. Each looks at a different part of the evidence and information surrounding a business.

01

Entity Clarity

Is it clear who the business is and what it does?

 02

Knowledge Completeness

Is the important information about the business sufficiently complete?

 03

Trust Evidence

Is there credible evidence supporting the claims being made?

 04

Technical Accessibility

Can important information be accessed and understood technically?

 05

Narrative Consistency

Does the business tell a consistent story across its digital presence?

 06

External Validation

Is the business supported by relevant evidence beyond its own website?
AN IMPORTANT DISTINCTION

We improve the signals. We don't promise the outcome.

There is an important difference between strengthening the information and evidence surrounding a business and claiming that this will make an AI system, search engine or third-party platform recommend or surface that business.

Our work focuses on what can be assessed and improved: the clarity, completeness, consistency, accessibility and supporting evidence of your digital representation.

We do not guarantee discovery, recommendation, ranking, citation, inclusion or visibility by any AI system, search engine or third-party service.

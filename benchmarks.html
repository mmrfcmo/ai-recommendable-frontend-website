Key Findings

🔴 Schema is the Biggest Gap

The average business scores just 48/100 on schema markup. Most websites have no structured data at all, making it the single highest-impact, lowest-effort fix available.

🔴 Trust Signals Are Weakest

At an average of 44/100, trust signals (reviews, testimonials, case studies) are the weakest area across all industries. Most businesses have social proof but do not display it effectively.

🟢 Legal & Financial Lead

Professional services firms (legal, financial) consistently score highest due to their structured content, clear entity signals, and established online presence.

🟢 Technical SEO Strongest

At 59/100, technical SEO is the strongest signal across all industries. Most businesses have basic meta tags, viewport settings, and HTTPS in place.

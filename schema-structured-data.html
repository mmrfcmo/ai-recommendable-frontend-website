What It Is

Make Your Business Readable by AI

Schema markup is structured data added to your website that helps AI systems understand exactly what your business is, what you offer, and how to reference you correctly.

Without it, AI systems have to guess — and they often get it wrong. With proper schema, you give AI a clear, unambiguous description of your business.

Every business we work with receives a full schema audit, implementation, and validation against Google's Rich Results Test.

What We Implement

Schema Types We Deploy

Organisation

Business name, logo, contact details, social profiles

LocalBusiness

Address, opening hours, geo-location, service area

FAQ

Question-answer pairs for AI extraction

Article

Blog posts, news, educational content

BreadcrumbList

Site navigation structure for AI

Service

Service offerings with descriptions and pricing

AI Governance: Who Should Be Responsible in Your Business?

As AI becomes more integrated into your business, you need clear governance structures to ensure it is used responsibly, effectively, and in compliance with regulations.

Why Governance Matters

AI can make mistakes, produce biased outcomes, or be used in ways that create risk. Without governance, these issues go unnoticed until they become problems.

Who Should Be Responsible

In a small business, AI governance might be the responsibility of the business owner or a senior manager. As you grow, consider creating an AI oversight role or committee.

Key Governance Responsibilities

Setting AI use policies and guidelines, reviewing AI tools before adoption, monitoring AI performance and outcomes, ensuring compliance with regulations, handling AI-related incidents or complaints, and reviewing and updating AI practices regularly.

Practical Governance for SMEs

You do not need a complex governance framework. Start with a simple AI use policy that covers what AI can and cannot be used for, how data should be handled, and what to do if something goes wrong.

Good AI governance protects your business, builds trust with customers, and ensures you get the most value from your AI investments.

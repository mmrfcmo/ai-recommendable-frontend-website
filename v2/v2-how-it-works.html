1

AI Visibility Assessment

The starting point. A prospect completes a 7-facet assessment scoring each area from 1-10.

7 Facets Assessed
  • AI Strategy
  • People & Skills
  • Processes & Automation
  • Data Readiness
  • Technology
  • Governance & Risk
  • Leadership & Culture
Scoring Band
  • 1-3: Foundation needed
  • 4-6: Implementation needed
  • 7-8: Optimisation ready
  • 9-10: Leadership stage
2

Proposal Builder

Open page →

Takes assessment scores and automatically selects the correct bands with pricing. Uses the 2-Tier Discount System:

Tier 1: Buy 2 Bands, Get 2 Free

Each facet has 4 bands. Pay for Band 0 + Band 1 and get Bands 2 + 3 free. Max £1,794 per facet.

Tier 2: Buy 5 Facets, Get 2 Free

If 5+ facets need work, total capped at £8,975. Saves up to 73% vs £33,544 full price.

Connected to: Client Proposal page — passes scores and band selections via URL params (?f1=2&f2=5&...&auto=1).

3

Client Proposal Page

Open page →

Two versions — editable (for you to configure) and read-only (for the client to view).

Editable Version

Select bands per facet, fill business context (pain points, manual data, goals), see live pricing, then generate the full proposal or open the read-only version.

Read-Only Version

Clean client-facing view with collapsible sections, full executive summary, rate card, investment breakdown, business context, and generator links.

Connected to: All 7 generators — the read-only page has a "Generate Deliverables" section with links that pass client name, industry, pain points, goals, and manual data to each generator via URL params.

4

7 Deliverable Generators

All connected via URL params

Each facet has its own generator that produces specific documents for that area. They auto-fill when called from the proposal page.

AI StrategyOpen

Generates strategy report, roadmap, opportunity analysis, gap assessment, use case catalogue, investment brief, and success framework.

People & SkillsOpen

Generates skills audit, upskilling plan, role framework, training materials, change readiness assessment, and team development plan.

Processes & AutomationOpen

Generates process maps, automation scripts, ROI framework, workflow documentation, and efficiency improvement plan.

Data ReadinessOpen

Generates data inventory, quality assessment, cleaning scripts, validation framework, pipeline design, governance plan, and strategy document.

TechnologyOpen

Generates stack audit, gap analysis, tool selection guide, integration plan, scalability assessment, and infrastructure roadmap.

Governance & RiskOpen

Generates acceptable use policy, privacy review, risk assessment, bias monitoring framework, audit plan, ethics board charter, and transparency report.

Leadership & CultureOpen

Generates business case, leadership deck, change management plan, communication strategy, culture assessment, and vision narrative.

URL param connection: Each generator accepts ?name=...&industry=...&pain=...&manual=...&goals=...&company=...&auto=1. When auto=1, it generates the documents automatically after 800ms.

One-Click Deliver

Open page →

The final step. With one click, generates all 7 generators in sequence and emails the complete deliverable pack to the client. Each generator is called with the client's name and industry, auto-generates, and the results are compiled and sent via Formspree to mmr1979@hotmail.co.uk.

Email endpoint: All pages use Formspree at https://formspree.io/f/mqpzqkdl to send proposals and deliverables.

Key insight: The entire system is held together by URL parameters. Every page reads ?name=...&industry=...&auto=1 from the URL and auto-fills its fields. This means the One-Click Deliver page can orchestrate the whole workflow by opening each generator URL with the right params, and each generator auto-generates without human intervention.

How Auto-Pilot Works

🔗
1. URL Parameters

Every page accepts ?name=...&industry=...&pain=...&auto=1 in the URL. No database needed.

⚡
2. Auto-Generate on Load

When auto=1 is present, the page waits 800ms for fields to populate, then runs the generate function automatically.

✉
3. Email Delivery

All pages send completed documents via Formspree to mmr1979@hotmail.co.uk. One-Click Deliver does this for all 7 generators in sequence.

Using AI to Streamline Email and Communication Overload

The average professional spends over three hours per day on email. For business owners, that number is often higher. AI tools can dramatically reduce the time you spend on email while improving the quality of your responses.

How AI Can Help with Email

Smart Filtering and Prioritisation

AI can learn what matters to you and surface important emails while filtering out newsletters, spam, and low-priority messages. Tools like SaneBox and Shortwave use AI to prioritise your inbox so you see what matters first.

Drafting Responses

AI writing assistants can draft email responses based on the context of the conversation. You review and send. This alone can cut email time by 50%. The key is using AI to generate a first draft, then adding your personal touch.

Summarising Long Threads

When you join a long email thread, AI can summarise what has been discussed and what decisions have been made. This saves you from reading dozens of messages to catch up.

Scheduling and Follow-Ups

AI can automatically schedule follow-up reminders, send follow-up emails when someone has not responded, and archive or categorise emails based on content.

A Practical Email Workflow

Start your day by reviewing AI-prioritised emails first. Respond to critical messages using AI drafts. Batch-process newsletters and non-urgent emails at a set time. Use AI to schedule follow-ups for anything requiring a response later.

The 80/20 Rule for Email

Most of your email value comes from 20% of your messages. Use AI to identify that 20% and handle it personally. Let AI draft responses for the routine 80%. This approach maintains personal connection where it matters while dramatically reducing time spent.

The goal is not to eliminate human interaction from email. It is to remove the mechanical work so you can focus on the conversations that actually matter.

Signal 1 of 6

Authority (Max: 20 points)

Evidence of Expertise and Recognition

What It Is

Authority measures how well your business is recognised as an expert in your field. AI systems look for citations, press mentions, backlinks from authoritative sources, and third-party recognition that confirms your expertise.

Why It Matters

When AI systems decide which business to recommend, they prioritise those with demonstrable authority. A business mentioned in industry publications, with strong backlinks and press coverage, is far more likely to be recommended over a competitor with no external recognition.

How the Scanner Checks It

The scanner analyses your website for citations, press mentions, backlink quality indicators, and references from authoritative third-party sources. It checks whether your business is referenced in industry publications, news articles, or recognised directories.

How to Improve

  • Build backlinks from reputable industry sites
  • Get featured in local or trade press
  • Display media mentions and press logos on your site
  • Earn citations from authoritative directories
  • Publish guest content on industry platforms
Signal 2 of 6

Trust (Max: 15 points)

Reviews, Testimonials and Social Proof

What It Is

Trust measures the visible credibility indicators on your website and across the web. Reviews, testimonials, case studies, awards, accreditations, client logos, and Trustpilot ratings all contribute to your trust signal score.

Why It Matters

AI systems are designed to recommend trustworthy businesses. A company with strong reviews, published case studies, and visible social proof is significantly more likely to be recommended than one with no visible trust indicators, regardless of service quality.

How the Scanner Checks It

The scanner looks for keywords like "testimonial", "review", "rating", "case study", "award", "certified", "accredited", "featured in", and checks for integration with platforms like Trustpilot.

How to Improve

  • Display testimonials prominently on your homepage
  • Create a dedicated case studies page
  • Link to your Trustpilot or Google Reviews profile
  • Showcase awards and accreditations
  • Add client logos to build instant credibility
Signal 3 of 6

Content (Max: 20 points)

Topic Coverage, Depth and Relevance

What It Is

Content measures how thoroughly your website covers the topics your customers care about. AI systems look for FAQs, blog articles, guides, service pages, and structured content that demonstrates expertise in your field.

Why It Matters

When a potential customer asks AI a question about your industry, the AI searches for the most comprehensive, authoritative answer. Businesses with shallow content get skipped. Those with deep, well-structured content get referenced and recommended.

How the Scanner Checks It

The scanner checks for FAQs, blog sections, guides, how-to content, lists, tables, and overall word count. It looks for evidence that your website provides genuine value rather than thin content.

How to Improve

  • Add an FAQ section answering common customer questions
  • Create service pages with detailed, original content
  • Publish regular blog articles on relevant topics
  • Include guides, tutorials, or how-to resources
  • Aim for a minimum of 500 words per key page
Signal 4 of 6

Entity Clarity (Max: 15 points)

Business Identity, NAP and Consistency

What It Is

Entity clarity measures how clearly AI systems can identify who your business is, what you do, and where you operate. It covers your business name, address, phone number (NAP), company description, founding story, and the clarity of your online identity.

Why It Matters

AI systems cross-reference your business information across multiple sources. If they find inconsistencies — different phone numbers, slightly different business names, conflicting addresses — they lose confidence in your entity. This directly reduces the likelihood of being recommended.

How the Scanner Checks It

The scanner checks for clear company descriptions, business history (founded/established dates), H1 tags, title tags, and NAP presence. It also cross-references your NAP against Google Business Profile and other directories.

How to Improve

  • Add a clear "who we are" statement to your homepage
  • Include founding date and location details
  • Ensure NAP is identical across all platforms
  • Add a descriptive H1 and title tag to every page
  • Use our NAP Checker tool to identify inconsistencies
Signal 5 of 6

Structure (Max: 20 points)

Schema.org Markup and Machine Readability

What It Is

Structure measures how well your website is organised for machine understanding. Schema.org markup (JSON-LD) tells AI systems exactly what your business is, what you offer, your location, your reviews, and your pricing. It is the language AI systems use to read your website.

Why It Matters

Without schema markup, AI systems have to guess what your business does. With schema, they know instantly. Properly structured data is one of the highest-impact, lowest-effort improvements you can make to your AI discoverability.

How the Scanner Checks It

The scanner looks for application/ld+json blocks, schema.org references, itemscope and itemtype attributes, and counts the number of structured data blocks on your website.

How to Improve

  • Add Organisation and LocalBusiness schema to your homepage
  • Implement Service schema on each service page
  • Add Review schema for testimonials
  • Use FAQ schema for question-and-answer content
  • Validate your schema using Google's Rich Results Test
Signal 6 of 6

Technical SEO (Max: 15 points)

Meta Tags, Mobile Optimisation and Site Health

What It Is

Technical SEO covers the foundational elements that make your website accessible and understandable to both AI systems and users. Meta descriptions, viewport settings, heading structures, HTTPS security, and mobile responsiveness all play a role.

Why It Matters

If AI systems cannot properly crawl and interpret your website, they cannot recommend it. Missing meta descriptions, broken heading structures, and poor mobile optimisation all reduce your chances of being discovered and recommended by AI platforms.

How the Scanner Checks It

The scanner checks for meta description tags, viewport meta tags, H1 heading presence, HTTPS security, and overall mobile readiness. It flags missing or improperly implemented technical elements.

How to Improve

  • Add unique meta descriptions to every page
  • Ensure your viewport meta tag is properly set
  • Use a clear H1-H3 heading hierarchy
  • Enable HTTPS across your entire site
  • Test mobile responsiveness using Google's Mobile-Friendly Test

AI Is Becoming Part of the Buying Journey.

When someone asks an AI platform:

  • "Who are the best accountants in London?"
  • "Which dentist should I use?"
  • "What is the best estate agent near me?"
  • "Which marketing agency would you recommend?"

the platform is no longer simply returning a traditional list of search results. It is interpreting the question, evaluating available information and generating a recommendation. This creates a new competitive environment: The AI Recommendation Economy.

Being Mentioned Is Not the Same as Being Recommended.

A business can appear in an AI-generated answer without the AI actually endorsing it.

"Businesses including A, B and C offer this service."

Mentioned — but not endorsed.

"I'd recommend Business A because..."

Actively recommended by the AI.

These are fundamentally different outcomes.

Methodology

How We Research AI Recommendations

AI recommendation research is different from traditional search measurement. AI outputs can change according to the prompt, context, location, model, date and information available to the system. A single AI response therefore should not be treated as definitive evidence. Our research uses systematic testing wherever possible.

STANDARDISED PROMPTS

Use consistent prompts when comparing businesses.

REPEATABLE TESTING

Repeat tests where appropriate.

MULTI-PLATFORM TESTING

Compare multiple AI platforms.

MULTI-QUERY TESTING

Test multiple customer intents.

TIME-STAMPED RESULTS

Record when tests were conducted.

COMPETITIVE COMPARISON

Measure businesses against relevant competitors.

TRANSPARENT SAMPLE SIZES

Always disclose businesses and prompts tested.

DOCUMENTED LIMITATIONS

Clearly state limitations.

Replication: where practical, findings are replicated before being treated as stronger evidence.

Creating Responsible AI Guidelines for Your Business

Every business using AI needs clear guidelines. These do not need to be lengthy legal documents. A simple set of principles and practices can help ensure AI is used responsibly and consistently.

Why You Need Guidelines

AI guidelines protect your business from legal and reputational risk. They help your team make consistent decisions about AI use. And they demonstrate to customers that you take responsible AI seriously.

What Good Guidelines Cover

Your guidelines should cover the types of AI your business will and will not use, data handling and privacy requirements, transparency about AI use with customers, human oversight requirements, incident reporting and response, and regular review and update procedures.

Creating Your Guidelines

Start with your values. What principles matter most to your business? Build your AI guidelines around these values. Involve your team in the process. Review and update your guidelines as AI technology evolves.

Implementing Guidelines

Guidelines only work if people know about them and follow them. Communicate your guidelines clearly. Provide training. And hold people accountable for following them.

Responsible AI is not a constraint. It is a competitive advantage that builds trust with customers and protects your business.
